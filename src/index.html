Conference Background

Event Overview

What if the history of an institution is not merely measured by the number of years it has existed, but by the lives it has transformed, the values it has nurtured and the generations it has empowered?

The Legacy Conclave 2026 is a prestigious national-level gathering organised by Shaheen Group of Institutions, Bidar, to recognise, honour and celebrate the remarkable contributions of educational institutions that have completed more than 50 years of dedicated service in the field of education.

The conference aims to bring together approximately 25 distinguished educational institutions from different parts of India that have played a significant role in educational development, social transformation and community empowerment over several decades.

These institutions represent a legacy of visionary leadership, commitment, perseverance and academic excellence. Their founders, successive generations of management and dedicated educators have contributed immensely to shaping generations and transforming society through education.

Institutional Greeting

Welcome Message

Dr. Abdul Qadeer

Chairman

Shaheen Group of Institutions, Bidar, Karnataka

Organiser & Host

It gives us immense pleasure and honour to welcome all the distinguished dignitaries, visionary educational leaders, eminent academicians, institutional founders, management representatives and delegates to The Legacy Conclave 2026, organised by Shaheen Group of Institutions, Bidar.

This conclave is a celebration of extraordinary journeys, enduring values and the remarkable contributions of educational institutions that have dedicated more than five decades to the noble cause of education and community development.

We are privileged to bring together approximately 25 pioneering educational institutions from different parts of India. Each institution carries a unique story of vision, sacrifice, perseverance and service. Their journeys reflect the commitment of visionary founders, successive generations of leadership and countless educators who have shaped the lives of thousands of students and contributed to the progress of society.

At Shaheen Group of Institutions, we strongly believe that the history of an institution is not merely measured by the number of years it has existed, but by the lives it has transformed, the values it has nurtured and the generations it has empowered.

The Legacy Conclave is therefore not just an occasion for felicitation; it is an opportunity to connect generations of educational leadership, exchange experiences, share institutional challenges and achievements, and inspire emerging institutions to build a stronger future.

We hope this gathering will create meaningful relationships, encourage new collaborations and establish a lasting network of educational institutions committed to excellence and social transformation. As we honour those who laid the foundations of educational excellence, let us also inspire those who will carry this mission forward.

Let us celebrate the legacy of the past, strengthen the bonds of the present and collectively shape the future of education.

With warm regards and best wishes,
Dr. Abdul Qadeer • Chairman, Shaheen Group of Institutions

Core Mission

Objectives of the Conference

Five foundational pillars guiding the deliberations, felicitations, and national roadmap.

01

Honouring Educational Legacies

Recognising and felicitating institutions that have completed more than 50 years of educational service with enduring excellence.

02

Celebrating Visionary Leadership

Acknowledging founders, management members and educational leaders whose vision established and sustained enduring institutions.

03

Knowledge Sharing & Exchange

Creating a collaborative platform for established institutions to share experiences, governance challenges, and pedagogical innovations.

04

Mentoring Emerging Institutions

Enabling legacy institutions to guide newer organizations in institutional governance, long-term sustainability, and academic excellence.

05

Building a Collaborative National Network

Establishing a permanent, national platform for institutional collaboration, academic partnerships, and collective leadership through LINN.

Proposed Major Outcome of the Conclave

The Legacy Institutions National Network (LINN)

A national platform connecting India's pioneering educational institutions.

The conclave will conclude with the historic announcement of a national network to facilitate enduring solidarity and collaborative evolution across India:

✓
Annual Meetings

Regular annual conventions of legacy institutions across India to assess educational progress.

✓
Best Practice Sharing

Continuous sharing of institutional experiences, pedagogy, and governance models.

✓
Mentorship for Emerging Institutions

Guiding and nurturing newer educational establishments in longevity and ethical stewardship.

✓
Academic Collaborations & Exchanges

Fostering joint research, faculty development, and nationwide student exchange initiatives.

✓
Documenting Institutional History

Archiving and publishing the inspiring histories and societal impacts of India's pioneering institutions.

✓
Collective Future Initiatives

United national action to shape the future policies and digital transformation of education.
